How JCC Leadership Works

Campus Representation & Coordination

JCC operates through a unique leadership structure where each campus Christian Union elects representatives who serve as both campus leaders and JCC coordinators.

Campus Leadership

Each campus elects its own Chairperson and Secretary who lead local CU activities while representing their campus at JCC meetings.

  • Chairperson: Overall campus CU leadership
  • Secretary: Administrative coordination and communication
  • Additional Leaders: Organizing secretary, treasurer, fellowship coordinators

JCC Coordination

All campus representatives meet regularly to coordinate activities, share resources, and maintain unity across all University of Nairobi campuses.

  • Weekly Meetings: Leadership coordination and planning
  • Joint Events: Coordinated campus-wide activities
  • Resource Sharing: Best practices and support systems

Two-Way Communication

Campus leaders communicate both with their local CU members and with JCC executive team, ensuring effective coordination at all levels.

Campus Autonomy

Each campus maintains its unique identity and local leadership while participating in the unified JCC structure.

Shared Vision

All campus leaders work together under common doctrinal foundation and unified vision for Christian campus ministry.
