JCC coordinates 8 Christian Union fellowships spread across all University of Nairobi campuses, from the CBD to the outskirts of Nairobi and beyond. Each CU operates independently under its own elected leadership while remaining connected to JCC for joint events, shared resources, and unified vision.

Whether you are a first-year student looking for a fellowship home or a continuing student wanting to deepen your faith walk, your campus CU is the right starting point. Find your campus below and take the first step.

What Happens at a Campus CU

Discover the activities and programs you'll find at your campus Christian Union

Fellowship Meetings

Weekly gatherings for worship, prayer, and Bible teaching open to all students.

Bible Study Groups

Smaller midweek groups that go deeper into Scripture together.

Outreach Programs

Regular campus and community outreach drives coordinated across campuses.

Leadership Training

JCC runs termly training for CU leaders drawn from all campuses.

How to Join Your Campus CU

Three simple steps to get started with your campus Christian Union

1

Find Your Campus

Find your campus above and click Visit Campus CU.

2

Attend a Meeting

Attend any fellowship meeting. No registration needed to visit.

3

Fill Membership Form

Fill the membership form on your campus page or speak to any CU leader.

Joining is free. All University of Nairobi registered students are welcome regardless of denomination.
