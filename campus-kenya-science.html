Kenya Science Campus CU Introduction

About Kenya Science Campus CU

Growing in Faith Along Ngong Road

Kenya Science Campus Christian Union serves students of the Faculty of Science and Technology at University of Nairobi. Located along Ngong Road, our CU provides a nurturing environment for technology and science students to develop their faith alongside their academic pursuits.

We are committed to creating a community where students can explore the intersection of faith and science, fostering intellectual growth while maintaining strong spiritual foundations. Our programs are designed to equip students to be Christian leaders in their respective technological and scientific fields.

Our Mission

To build a vibrant Christian community that supports academic excellence in science and technology fields.
