Upper Kabete Campus CU Introduction

About Upper Kabete Campus CU

Growing in Faith Along Kapenguria Road

Upper Kabete Campus Christian Union serves students of the College of Agriculture and Veterinary Sciences at University of Nairobi. Located along Kapenguria Road, our CU provides a nurturing environment for agriculture and veterinary students to grow in their faith while pursuing their academic excellence.

We are passionate about creating an environment where future agricultural and veterinary professionals can integrate their Christian faith with their professional training. Our programs focus on stewardship, ethical practices, and building a strong foundation for careers grounded in Christian service and care for creation.

Our Focus

Preparing agricultural professionals who will serve with faith, integrity, and care for God's creation.
